# Editorial workflow

## Every morning

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Review the **High priority** papers first.
3. Open paper detail pages for anything that looks solicitation-worthy.
4. Use the contact panel to decide whether the corresponding-author details are ready to use.

## Every Friday

1. Open **Digests**.
2. Review the weekly digest and spot recurring themes.
3. Check whether the rubric is over-weighting or under-weighting any topic.

## When the journal focus shifts

1. Open **Rubric**.
2. Save a new draft rather than editing the active version in place.
3. Preview recent papers.
4. Activate only when the changes look sensible.

## Good rubric hygiene

- change one thing at a time when calibrating
- keep notes on why each draft exists
- avoid too many broad keyword boosts
- prefer methodology and topic signals over hype words
- keep contactability weighted, but not high enough to bury better science
